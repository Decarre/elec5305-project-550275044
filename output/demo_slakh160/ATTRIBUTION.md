# Audio attribution

The WAV excerpts use synthetic audio from Slakh2100-redux by Ethan Manilow, Gordon Wichern, Prem Seetharaman and Jonathan Le Roux (2019). [Original dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/4599666) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The project resampled the original mixes to 16 kHz and extracted two five-second validation excerpts. success.wav uses Track01661 at 255 seconds. failure.wav uses Track01654 at 100 seconds. These same excerpts appear in the demonstration video.
